### Key Insights
* Revenue is relatively stable across different payment methods.
* Minivans and sedans are strong-performing vehicle types.
* About 1 in 3 rides is cancelled or ends as a no-show.
* Driver delays and high prices are major reasons for cancellations.
* The average rating of 2.98/5 shows low customer satisfaction.
* Some high-demand areas may need more driver coverage.

### Recommendations
* Improve driver availability and dispatch speed.
* Review pricing during peak hours.
* Improve driver customer-service training.
* Increase driver coverage in high-demand areas.
* Reduce no-shows through reminders and incentives.

### Business Outcome
The dashboard helps identify opportunities to:
* Reduce ride cancellations
* Improve driver performance
* Increase driver availability
* Improve customer satisfaction
  
### Conclusion
Reduce Cancellations → Improve Driver Performance → Increase Customer Satisfaction.
